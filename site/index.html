Why AgentDeck

23 providers at a glance

Limits, balances and local statistics stay distinct. A limit the provider doesn't report is never invented, and estimates are always labelled.

Accounts that don't collide

Each extra Claude Code, Codex or Grok account gets its own home and Keychain item. MCP servers, hooks and plugins are shared; credentials never are.

Switch before you stall

Manual or automatic switching at your threshold, ranked by headroom and reset time. Running sessions keep their account.

Your setup, untouched

Uses the CLIs you already have (mise first). Nothing is installed or duplicated; logins, settings and histories stay where they are.

Self-contained

The app carries its own Python and ripgrep for the collectors. No extra installs.

Private by default

Usage stays on your Mac. Optional cross-device totals export numbers only — never sign-ins or conversations.

Install

brew install --cask jdk1021/tap/agentdeck
  1. Or download the DMG from Releases and drag AgentDeck to Applications.
  2. First launch: the app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.
  3. Then run agentdeck refresh or just open the menu bar panel.
